6 to 8 Month Home Delivery Lifecycle

Five clear stages take you from idea to keys in your hand.

  1. Consultation

    We meet, discuss your budget and choose the best plot and design.

  2. Design & Approvals

    Architectural drawings are finalised and map approval is obtained.

  3. Foundation & Structure

    Excavation, foundation, columns and slabs are built and inspected.

  4. Finishing

    Plaster, tiles, electrical, plumbing, woodwork and paint are completed.

  5. Handover

    Final inspection, cleaning and keys handed to you with all documents.

Our Policy

  • Fixed written pricing with no hidden charges.
  • Only verified materials and approved contractors.
  • Progress updates and site photos every week.
  • Delivery within the promised 6 to 8 months.
  • Support and repair of defects after handover.
